🎹 Piano Note Trainer

Play the note shown on the staff on your piano. The app listens through the microphone.

Turn on the microphone and calibrate to play a real piano, or just use the on-screen keyboard.
Note set
On-screen keyboard
Click, tap, or use your computer keys: A S D F G H J K (white) and W E T Y U (black).
Note sets (from your teacher) 
Format (plain text, one pattern per line):
title: Treble C to B
clef: treble        // treble, bass or auto
order: random       // random, sequence or lines
rounds: 20          // how many notes (random) or lines (lines)
repeat: 1           // sequence only
C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4
C4-B4               // range of white keys
F#4 Bb3             // sharps (#) and flats (b)
Blank lines and anything after // are ignored. Octave 4 starts at middle C.
order: random picks from all notes; sequence plays each line in order; lines picks random lines and plays each through.

Tip: if the next note is the same as the last, let the key go (or strike it again firmly) so the app hears a new note. Best used with the page open in its own browser tab.
